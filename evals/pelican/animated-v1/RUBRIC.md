# Frozen animation review criteria

This is one descriptive A/B pair, not a statistical performance claim. Installation, loading, implicit invocation, native task completion, technical validity, visual quality and motion validity are distinct observations. Do not change these criteria after seeing either output.

Review the untouched full animation for at least two cycles, at full and half size, and inspect multiple phases. A moving background or rotating wheels alone do not satisfy the task. Technical checks do not establish visual correctness.

Score each axis independently from 0 (absent/broken), 1 (major defects), 2 (partly coherent with visible defects), 3 (mostly coherent with small defects), to 4 (consistently coherent across the inspected cycle). Give concrete visible evidence and phase/time for every defect. No preset pass threshold or desired winning arm.

1. Pelican recognition and physical form, including bill/pouch, body, limbs and continuity.
2. Bicycle structure, including frame, fork, wheel hubs, crank, pedals and drivetrain depiction.
3. Riding contacts: support on saddle, foot-to-pedal attachment and wing-to-grip contact across the cycle.
4. Motion kinematics: leg articulation, pedal/crank relationship and consistent wheel motion; no detachment, impossible extension or abrupt reversal.
5. Spatial continuity and occlusion throughout motion: hidden lines, front/back ordering and connected parts.
6. Temporal execution: autonomous playback, six-second repetition, smoothness and loop join; distinguish intended moving scenery from mechanism motion.
7. Whole-image visual execution and readability at full/half size.

Preserve failures and time/tool use. If reviewed by the coordinator who knows arm labels, call it an unblinded review. A future independent blind packet must expose only neutral animation files/videos and these criteria, excluding source history, Skill names and arm mapping. Do not reuse static-run judgments or claim new independent judging without an actual new review.
