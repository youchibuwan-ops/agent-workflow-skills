# Frozen pelican SVG A/B rubric

One predeclared pair, not a statistical effect estimate or a friend's study.
Both groups receive exactly `prompt.txt` and `TOOLS.md`, with no reference images.
This tests the available Skill's ordinary implicit use, not an explicitly forced
invocation. B enables only the released SVG Skill; discovery Skill is not another
treatment. Loading and observable execution are reported separately from quality.

## Technical eligibility

Preserve a missing, invalid, partial or timed-out output as a failure; do not replace
it with a favorable retry. Independently parse and render the actual output.svg.
Check 1200x900 canvas/viewBox, vector editability, absence of scripts, external
render dependencies and raster images. PNG presence is separate from SVG validity.
Record budget/contamination failures separately. An available partial drawing may
still be visually scored, with its completion failure attached.

## Blind visual scoring: five axes, each integer 0–4 (total 20)

Use the rendered pixels, not author confidence, trace length or decoration count.
For each axis: 0 absent/unusable; 1 major errors; 2 recognizable but material flaws;
3 coherent with minor flaws; 4 clear and well resolved. Give concrete image locations
and observable reasons. Cartoon simplification is allowed; photo realism is not required.

1. **Pelican recognition/form:** distinctive long bill and pouch, bird/head/neck/body/
   wing/feet relationships read coherently; avoid rewarding generic bird decoration.
2. **Bicycle structure:** wheel/hub/frame/fork/steering/crank/pedal relationships form
   a coherent bicycle; essential members and supports do not float or attach absurdly.
3. **Riding interaction:** believable readable support/seat and limb-to-cycle contact;
   the bird reads as riding, not pasted over or intersecting a bicycle. Anthropomorphic
   posing is allowed without requiring impossible precise zoological anatomy.
4. **Spatial continuity/occlusion:** consistent projection, scale, continuous entities,
   intersections, front/back ordering and visible lines; occluded lines do not show through.
5. **Whole-image execution:** integrated composition, useful negative space, legibility,
   consistent palette/line/material language, complete contours and no accidental clipping.

## Blind procedure and interpretation

Before viewing outputs, assign neutral random IDs; keep the mapping outside reviewer
access. Strip process-revealing comments/metadata from blind copies only; originals
remain unchanged. Render originals/copies with the same resvg at 1200x900 and 600x450;
require pixel equality before judging. No arm names, source code, process logs, model
claims or desired answer go to judges. Each judge sees each PNG independently before
the pair, then provides five scores/reasons and a preference/tie without guessing arms.
Two fresh independent xhigh judges see reversed presentation order. Freeze and hash
both verdicts before unblinding. Do not feed their feedback back to either actor.

Report both judgments; disagreements remain visible. Aggregate with the mean total
only as a descriptive result; a tie or B loss is acceptable. No post-hoc thresholds,
best-of sampling or stopping after a desired winner. Even a B win is a single paired
author experiment, not proof of causality, reliability or a general performance gain.
If B never reads/applies its Skill, report that explicitly; do not call it a test of
the instructed workflow's successful execution. No forced-call rerun replaces this pair.
