## Structure judgment from the inspected images

- These are two shaped wooden solids, not two flat strips. Each has a broad face and real depth. The inner profiles have jaw/line reliefs and a middle bearing region; the outer faces have transverse wire seats. Their actual dimensions vary among candidates.
- The metal part is bent round wire. Its middle is a helical coil with an axis across the clip's width/depth; a side projection shows the coil opening and overlapping turns. A single flat decorative ring or an added rivet would misdescribe the inspected construction.
- From the coil, a long wire segment runs toward the jaw region and bends across an outer transverse groove. The complementary segment belongs to the opposite arm. The iFixit separated spring, one-arm attachment, before view and assembled view together support this relation; the single side photo alone would not reveal the full path.
- In a side image, the near diagonal wire/coil rim lies in front of portions of the wood; the return/end segment turns around the wood edge into its groove. The far segment is partly occluded. The future SVG must respect those changes of depth instead of drawing both complete long legs over the front face or making a wire end stop freely on unnotched wood. The observed outside segment is a seated wire, not a through-screw.
- Closed jaws and spread handles coexist in the assembled examples. Do not mistake the rear gap for a broken clip, or use the disassembled/glued craft geometry as the closed-state silhouette.

These are qualitative visible/common relations, not an engineering certification or an exact reconstruction of a named product. No force, hidden bearing tolerances, unseen rear profile or precise coil turn count is asserted.

## Final scope / decision and unknowns

Reference research and a qualitative connection judgment are complete for this test; drawing remains stopped as requested. The iFixit same-guide sequence is the preferred structural set; Commons supplies clearer material/depth context. eBay supplies a near-side complementary example but its proportions may not be grafted onto the others.

**No asset or precision geometry is approved.** A truly orthographic side photograph of the selected ordinary specimen has not been established by this finite search. Thickness ratios, far groove depth, wire diameter, exact coil turns and hidden contact surfaces remain unmeasured. The frame's side-versus-broad-face ambiguity was explicitly checked by opening candidates, not resolved from filenames. If proceeding to a dimensionally faithful SVG, first lock one specimen and target size, then obtain its suitable side/depth evidence (or explicitly document bounded inference after further targeted search); do not declare those missing dimensions known. The qualitative mechanism is sufficiently supported to plan a difficult local spring-plus-adjacent-wood study, but that study and its actual rendering review have not happened.
