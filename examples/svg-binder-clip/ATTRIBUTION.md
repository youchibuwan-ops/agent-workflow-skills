`support-axial.jpg`: **Binder clip (3767489926).jpg**, smartel, 2009.
[Source and attribution](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Binder_clip_(3767489926).jpg),
[CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/).
Original 4752×3168 image resized proportionally to 1920×1280 and JPEG re-encoded.
The oblique mouth view separates opposing rolled sheet edges and open sections.
Within each visible row it also constrains relative segment lengths, the wider
central root, shorter free edge and entry gaps, with occlusion/projection error.
These relationships help challenge a misreading of the target; exact dimensions
are not transferred between specimens. The filename does not establish an axial
or orthographic camera angle. Its selected dimensional role concerns the body's
central lip and end rolls. Handle interfaces can be inspected here, but the
foreshortened handle projection alone cannot establish planarity, depth bend or
intrinsic length; complete complementary handle views are required.
